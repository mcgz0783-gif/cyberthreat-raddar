# Animation Script: Mastering Linux with CyberHawk UG

## Video Title: The Command Line Chronicles: Mastering Linux
**Target Audience:** Cybersecurity Students & Enthusiasts
**Duration:** 2-3 Minutes (Animated Short)

---

## Scene 1: The Matrix Moment
**Visual:** A dark room. A glowing green cursor blinks on a monitor. A hooded figure (CyberHawk UG Avatar) sits at the desk.
**Audio (Voice Over):** "In the world of cybersecurity, the GUI is a luxury. The command line? That's the reality."
**Action:** The camera zooms into the screen. The text "Mastering Linux" appears in a hacker-style font.

## Scene 2: Navigation & Search
**Visual:** A sprawling, isometric city made of folders and files.
**Audio (VO):** "Navigation is your first weapon. `ls` to see, `cd` to move. But to find the needles in the haystack? You need `grep` and `find`."
**Action:** The CyberHawk avatar throws a glowing 'grep' shuriken that highlights specific files in the folder-city.
**Text Overlay:** `find /etc -type f | xargs grep "secret"`

## Scene 3: The Shield (Permissions)
**Visual:** A large, medieval gate with a numeric lock (755).
**Audio (VO):** "Permissions are the gatekeepers. `chmod` and `chown` define who enters and who is cast out. Master the numbers, or the system remains closed."
**Action:** The avatar types `chmod 700` and the gate glows red, locking out shadow figures.
**Text Overlay:** `chmod 700 /home/user/.ssh`

## Scene 4: The Pulse (Networking)
**Visual:** A glowing map of network connections (pulses of light moving between nodes).
**Audio (VO):** "Your terminal is a radar. `ip addr` to find yourself, `nmap` to find the others. In the silence of the wire, `tcpdump` reveals the truth."
**Action:** The avatar scans the map with a sonar pulse labeled `nmap`.
**Text Overlay:** `nmap -sC -sV target.ip`

## Scene 5: Conclusion & CTA
**Visual:** The CyberHawk UG logo appears. The avatar stands on top of a mountain of terminal windows.
**Audio (VO):** "Linux isn't just an OS; it's a superpower. Start mastering it today at CyberHawk UG."
**Action:** Link to the blog appears: `cyberhawk-ug.store/blog`

---

## Storyboard Summary:
1. **Intro:** Hacker aesthetic, high energy.
2. **Body:** 3 core pillars (Navigation, Permissions, Networking) visualized as physical interactions.
3. **Outro:** Brand reinforcement and call to action.
